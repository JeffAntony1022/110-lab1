As a player, I should be able to see the day's cost-per-glass and my current assets before I decide anything, so I can choose how many glasses to make, how many advertising signs to buy, and what price to charge.

As a player, I should be able to input how many supplies to buy at the day's prices, so I can stock my inventory and control my spending against my cash balance.

As a player, I should be able to see the EOD results (cups sold, supplies remaining, and current cash balance), so I can track my profit and plan my strategy for the next day accordingly.
